Executive Summary & 3D ULPIN Encoding Standard:

Extends India's 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar 2D cadastre into a robust 3D volumetric indexing standard for multi-storey apartments, subsurface metro/utility tunnels, and regulated air-rights corridors.

3D-ULPIN = IN-[14-Digit Base Parcel]-[Strata Zone]-[Floor Level]-[Volumetric Unit ID]-[Modulo97 Checksum]
Example: IN-2187-4930-1049-A-F12-U1204-K8 (Floor 12 • Apartment #1204)
